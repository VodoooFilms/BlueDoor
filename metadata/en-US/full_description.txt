Bluedoor is a local workspace for reviewing and protecting private documents.
On-device models suggest sensitive spans; you review the map before export.
Models are downloaded only when you request them, from pinned sources with
SHA-256 verification. Documents are not uploaded. There are no application
accounts, advertising, analytics or remote crash reports.

Android beta: detection can miss sensitive details. Review every page, including
images and indirect identifiers, before sharing. This is pseudonymization,
not a guarantee of anonymity. Current OCR dependencies block F-Droid inclusion;
this listing is preparation, not a claim of availability in F-Droid.
